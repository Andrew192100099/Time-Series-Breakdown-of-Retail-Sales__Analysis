Tagline: From raw weekly sales → to actionable retail forecasting insights.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This project performs **time series analysis** on the Walmart Retail Sales dataset.  
The objective is to clean, transform, and analyze weekly sales data to uncover 
trends, seasonality, and anomalies, while generating insights to support retail 
business decision-making.

Dataset & Context: Walmart Sales Forecast Dataset (Kaggle).  
Main Techniques: Time series decomposition, correlation analysis, feature 
engineering, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).  

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DELIVERABLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I.   Data cleaning (handling nulls, duplicates, and missing values).  
II.  Feature engineering (date parsing, extracting week, month, year).  
III. Time series decomposition into trend, seasonality, and residuals.  
IV.  Exploratory analysis of sales across stores and departments.  
V.   Comparison of holiday vs. non-holiday sales performance.  
VI.  Correlation of sales with CPI, temperature, and unemployment.  
VII. Visualization of patterns, correlations, and decomposition results.  

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY ANALYTICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
✔ Identified **seasonal patterns** in weekly sales.  
✔ Detected **holiday-driven spikes** and their impact on revenue.  
✔ Long-term **trend analysis** for store and department performance.  
✔ Correlation findings: unemployment rate and CPI affect sales.  
✔ Residual analysis explained fluctuations not tied to trends.  

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VISUALIZATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Line chart — Weekly sales trends across stores.  
2. Decomposition plots — Trend, seasonality, and residuals.  
3. Heatmap — Store vs. weekly sales distribution.  
4. Holiday effect charts — Sales comparison with/without holidays.  
5. Correlation matrix — Macroeconomic indicators vs. sales.
