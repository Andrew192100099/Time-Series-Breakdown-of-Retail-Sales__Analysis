# Time-Series-Breakdown-of-Retail-Sales__Analysis
***How do retail sales behave over time?***
____________________

## 🌟 Project Story

**It began with a challenge:** ***“Can we uncover the hidden story behind Walmart’s retail sales over time?”***

I was handed four datasets — `train`, `stores`, `features`, and `test`. At first, they looked like a pile of spreadsheets, nothing more.
But beneath those rows and columns hid a powerful story of customer demand, seasonal shifts, and economic impact.

**I began piecing together the puzzle:**

*📅 Dates became my timeline.*

*🏬 Stores revealed differences in performance.*

*🛒 Features added hidden context like holidays and fuel prices.*

*💵 Weekly Sales became the heartbeat I had to track and forecast.*

With each step, the numbers started talking. Trends appeared. Patterns whispered secrets. And slowly, the retail landscape unfolded before my eyes.
_____________________________

## ❓ Business Questions We Asked

**Overall Trend:** *Are sales growing, declining, or stable?*

**Seasonality:** *Do holidays and seasons create predictable spikes?*

**Store Performance:** *Which stores consistently lead in sales?*

**External Factors:** *How do gas prices, markdowns, and unemployment affect revenue?*

**Forecasting:** *Can we make simple predictions to support planning?*
______________________

## 🛠️ How We Tackled It

**🔄 Merged & Cleaned Data →** *Combined train, features, and stores into a single timeline.*

**📈 Plotted Trends →** *Overall revenue curves showed clear growth phases and seasonal peaks.*

**🎯 Decomposed Series →** *Broke sales into trend, seasonality, residuals to isolate patterns.*

**🏬 Store Breakdown →** *Ranked stores by performance to reveal regional winners.*

**🎉 Holidays & Features →** *Linked holiday weeks and economic data to sharp sales spikes.*

**🔮 Forecasting →** *Used rolling means and exponential smoothing for a first predictive model.*
